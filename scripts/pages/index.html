Video upscaling to 8K

Motion Video Generator

Background Generator

Unlimited original backgrounds: gradients, aurora, bokeh, waves, geometric, mesh, textures, patterns, starfields and topography. PNG, JPG, and editable SVG/EPS vectors up to 8K.

Image to Vector

PNG, JPG and WebP traced into editable SVG, EPS and AI artwork with colour-quantisation and contour tracing that keeps real vector structure.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download vecmotion {{VERSION}} for Android above and let the APK finish downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your browser’s downloads tray or the Files app.
  3. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser — tap Settings, enable Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. Future updates install in place over this build; your data stays.

Verify your download

The release is signed with the vecmotion sideload key and every build publishes its SHA-256 next to the APK. Check yours matches before installing: 

SHA-256  {{SHA256}}

# Linux / macOS / Android (Termux)
sha256sum vecmotion-{{VERSION}}-release.apk

# Windows (PowerShell)
Get-FileHash vecmotion-{{VERSION}}-release.apk -Algorithm SHA256

# Expected value (also in SHA256SUMS.txt on the release):
